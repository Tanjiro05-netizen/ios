# Content and rights register

Last audited: 29 July 2026. This is a working evidence register, not a claim
that unresolved content is cleared for public commercial distribution.

## Imported dialectics package

Source package: `Marxist_Info_App_Content_Pack` version 1.0.0, supplied by the
project owner from `Marxist_Info_Course_Content_Only_v1.0`. The native importer
converts the source into one versioned learner package and retains source IDs.

| Record | Native content version | Current rights state | Current academic state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PHI111 — Hegelian Dialectics I | 2.0.0 | `needs_review` | `not_completed` |
| PHI211 — Marx's Dialectical Method | 1.0.0 | `needs_review` | `not_completed` |

The learner package includes 2 Study Guides, 2 Reading Guides, 103 glossary
terms, 57 bibliography records, 11 assignments with public rubrics, and 38
candidate-exam prompts. It includes no learner coursebook PDF. Quotations,
editions, translations, and bibliography entries require territory-specific
review even when the surrounding teaching prose was supplied by the owner.

Two examiner-only marking guides are represented only by restricted metadata:
`PHI111-MARKING-GUIDE` and `PHI211-MARKING-GUIDE`. Their source PDFs/Markdown,
hashes, and answer material remain outside source control and the learner app
target under `RestrictedCourseContent/`.

edition/translator where relevant, licence or permission, attribution text,
territories, expiry, reviewer, and sign-off date. Do not infer permission from
online availability or public-domain status in one country.

## Release rule

Draft status must remain visible until both intellectual accuracy and rights
have been reviewed. A closed expert beta does not remove the obligation to have
rights to distribute the content to testers. Anything without sufficient
evidence must be removed from that build or licensed before invitation.
