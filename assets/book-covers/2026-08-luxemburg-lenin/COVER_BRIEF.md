# Luxemburg and Lenin cover system

## Shared production rules

- Final size: 1200 x 1800 PNG (2:3 portrait).
- Keep faces, symbols, and all typography at least 9% inside every edge.
- Create the artwork with the built-in image generator, one distinct call per book.
- Ask the generator for artwork only: no words, letters, captions, logos, signatures, or watermark.
- Add all typography deterministically after generation so every title and author is exact.
- Text hierarchy: author in small tracked uppercase near the top; title in large high-contrast serif type; edition line only where required.
- Text must remain legible on a 136 x 200 mobile thumbnail.
- Shared medium: archival political poster, restrained linocut/photomontage, coarse newsprint grain, historically grounded objects and settings.
- Avoid invented quotations, political slogans, publisher marks, contemporary objects, generic stock imagery, and photorealistic copies of existing covers.
- Preserve each generated source artwork and the final composited cover. Never overwrite an existing asset.

## Rosa Luxemburg

- Palette: charcoal, warm ivory, burgundy, muted Prussian blue.
- Mood: intellectually forceful, humane, internationalist; avoid sentimental portraiture.
- Final author line: `ROSA LUXEMBURG`.

## V. I. Lenin

- Palette: charcoal, warm ivory, orange-red, restrained black.
- Mood: urgent, analytical, revolutionary; avoid triumphalist propaganda pastiche.
- Final author line: `V. I. LENIN`.

## Publishing boundary

- Generate six Luxemburg covers, but `The National Question` is a local unpublished draft only.
- Publish the other five Luxemburg books as new catalog records.
- Publish twelve Lenin books as new catalog records.
- Do not update any existing catalog record or storage object.
- Do not change Marx or Engels books or covers.
