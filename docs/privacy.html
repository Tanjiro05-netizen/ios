Privacy policy

This policy describes the information processed by Marxist Library and the choices available to users.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the Marxist Library mobile application and these support pages. The application provides a digital library, audiobook playback, article access, reading tools, optional account synchronization, and—in a later release—community features.

Data controller: To be supplied by the account holder before App Store submission.

2. Information we process

Account information Email address, account identifier, username or display name, authentication provider, and optional profile information.
Sign in with Apple Apple supplies an identity credential and may supply your name and either your email address or a private relay address. Apple normally provides the name only on the first authorization.
Reading activity When synchronization is enabled, book or chapter identifiers, reading position, completion progress, update time, and saved quote or highlight text.
Notifications A device push token and notification preferences when you enable notifications.
Support information Information you choose to include when asking for help. Please do not send passwords, private keys, or unnecessary sensitive information.
Community content Forum posts, comments, reactions, reports, and moderation records only when community features are enabled. The forum is not active in the first public release.

We do not currently use third-party advertising, sell personal data, or track activity across unrelated apps and websites.

3. Why we use it

  • Authenticate accounts and restore signed-in sessions.
  • Synchronize reading progress and saved quotes across supported devices.
  • Deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • Protect the service, prevent misuse, and diagnose operational failures.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce service rules where necessary.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing is based on providing the service you request, your consent, legitimate operational and security interests, or legal obligations.

4. Information kept on your device

Guest-session information, appearance preferences, downloaded EPUB files, cached covers and articles, playback state, and some offline reading metadata can remain locally on your device. Local information is not part of the App Store privacy label unless it is transmitted off the device.

Removing the app normally removes its local container. Files exported outside the app must be removed separately.

5. Services that help operate the app

Supabase

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and server-function infrastructure. It processes information on our behalf to operate accounts and synchronization.

Apple

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, TestFlight, and push-notification delivery. Apple processes information under its own agreements and privacy policy.

Substack and external links

The app can retrieve a publication feed and open articles or other links outside the app. The destination service receives ordinary connection information when you open it and applies its own privacy policy.

GitHub Pages

These public information pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard network and server-log information when the pages are requested. This site does not add analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and synchronized reading information is retained while your account remains active or while needed to provide the requested service. Operational records may be retained longer where required for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

You can permanently delete a signed-in account from the iOS app under Profile → Delete Account. The deletion workflow removes the account, profile, synchronized reading progress, saved quotes, push tokens, and associated user files, and clears related local account information. Learn more on the Account & Data page.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

  • Disable notifications in system settings.
  • Use guest mode for supported local reading features.
  • Sign out without deleting the account.
  • Delete the account and synchronized information from inside the app.

8. Children

The app is not designed for children and is intended for readers aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for children who cannot legally consent to the relevant processing. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been provided improperly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the app develops. Material changes will be reflected by a new date on this page and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. The policy will be reviewed before community features, analytics, advertising, payments, or new processors are introduced.

10. Contact

Private support email: To be supplied before public App Store submission.

For non-sensitive technical issues during development, use the project issue tracker. Never post account details or private information in a public issue.
