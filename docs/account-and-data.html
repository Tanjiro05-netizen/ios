Account

Account and data

Information about signing out, deleting an account, and making a privacy request.

Updated 29 July 2026 Deletion available in iOS
On this page Sign out Delete an account What is removed Guest data Other requests If deletion fails

1. Signing out

Signing out removes the active session from the device but does not delete the account or synchronized information. You can sign in again later and restore information associated with the same account.

2. Permanently deleting an account

In the iOS app:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Choose Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation and choose Delete Account again.

This action cannot be undone. A new account created later will not restore deleted synchronized information.

3. What deletion removes

  • The authentication account and profile.
  • Synchronized reading progress and saved quote records.
  • Push-notification tokens associated with the account.
  • User-owned files stored for the account.
  • Associated Keychain, preferences, and app-group account data on the device completing deletion.
  • Account-scoped local Study Center progress, attempts, review records, saved items, achievements, and written drafts on the device completing deletion.

Information may be retained only where legally required or necessary to resolve security incidents and disputes. The public forum is disabled in the first release; before it opens, the policy will explain how deletion affects posts needed to preserve discussion integrity or moderation records.

4. Guest and offline information

Guest reading and Study Center information is stored locally. Study Center progress is not uploaded or synchronized in this beta. Removing the app normally removes its local container, although files exported elsewhere remain under your control.

5. Access, correction, and other requests

You may request a copy of personal information, correction of inaccurate information, restriction, objection, or deletion where applicable. Send identity-sensitive requests to Modernmarxist05@gmail.com; never submit them through a public issue.

6. If deletion fails

Keep the app installed, confirm the device is online, sign in again if the session expired, and retry the deletion action. Do not publish account information in the GitHub issue tracker. Contact Modernmarxist05@gmail.com with only the minimum information needed to locate the request.

Read the complete Privacy Policy or return to Support.

Account deletion is permanent. Signing out is reversible.
