Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to read?

No. Supported library browsing and local reading can be used as a guest. An account is needed when you want reading progress and saved quotes synchronized across supported devices.

Why has my reading progress not appeared on another device?

Confirm that both devices use the same signed-in account and have an internet connection. Open the book once on the first device, allow synchronization to finish, and then refresh the library on the second device. Guest progress remains local until it is merged into a signed-in account.

How do I download a book for offline reading?

Open the book’s detail page and use the available download or read-offline action. Availability depends on the formats provided for that title. Keep the app installed to retain files stored inside its local container.

Why does audio stop or fail to begin?

Check the network connection, confirm the selected title has a valid audio source, and try reopening the player. Background playback should continue when the device is locked; system battery or audio-output settings can still interrupt playback.

How do I turn notifications off?

Open iOS Settings, choose Notifications, select Marxist Library, and disable the notification types you no longer want. The app also stores notification preferences where those controls are available.

How do I delete my account?

In the iOS app, open Profile and choose Delete Account. Confirming permanently removes the account and associated synchronized data. See the Account & Data page for the full explanation.

Where can I report a technical bug during development?

Use the public GitHub issue tracker for non-sensitive technical reports. Do not include your email address, account identifiers, screenshots containing personal information, or authentication details.

Can I report community content?

The community forum is not active in the first public release. Before it opens, reporting, blocking, moderation review, and a private safety contact will be available.

Never post passwords, private relay addresses, access tokens, or other sensitive account information in a public issue.
